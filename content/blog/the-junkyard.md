The junkyard, never given a name, was a central location for a lot of things. A small caern for an unusually organized pack of Bone-Gnawers. 

While out in yard today, going back and forth behind the lawn mower, my mind was wandering. For reasons unknown to myself (and probably anybody else), a connection was made and I realized the inspiration for the place. 

Let's think about it:

- A hidden mobile home.
- Multiple secret entrances.
- Hidden behind a working scrap yard. 

Yup, it's the [The Three Investigators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Three_Investigators), a series I read probably 10-20 books as a kid. I remember reading them at the tail end of my enjoyment of the [Hardy Boys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hardy_Boys) (still have 40 of those in a box), which ended around 5th or 6th grade. 

While the Hardy Boys books were inherited from my dad or bought for me (his copies pre-date the editing to remove stereotypes - which I didn't notice as a kid, but they also had a lot less than some of the other series). My introduction to the *Three Investigators* was around the same time I encountered [Tom Swift](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Swift). Thanks to the public library in New London, Wisconsin. I'm now wonder how much my Sons of Ether or Void Engineers are influenced by *Tom Swift*.
