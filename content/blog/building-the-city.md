There were a lot of sources for this campaign. When we started, it was with not much in terms of books or source material. The Web was new and there were some pages, but I really struggled with "the story".

So, without realizing it what I really did was run an old school sandbox style game rather than an actual series of stories with chapters and arcs and all the stuff that was trendy then. 

I'd throw out all kinds of things and based on the what the players did, we built something fun together. Did I always do the best, no. But, I also allowed the players to do whatever they wanted with their characters and some fared better than others.

This did make prep a bit challenging sometimes. There's a reason we had so many people named John around. If I needed a name and quickly, well, John. 

I can't trace how many things really influenced what we created. Stereotypes, books, movies, songs, and more was fodder. Current events, government reports, historical events, that list keeps growing. 

The City was never normal, it just was. I never did a map, we worked by districts and even those were flexible. A hell of a lot of fun, doing creativity on the fly.

So, checkout [Sources](/sources/), see what's there and, maybe, it will grow as I remember more. After all, even today, I still get inspired.
